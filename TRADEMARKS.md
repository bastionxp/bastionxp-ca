real thing, because it is security software and a modified build could
put its users at risk.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project.** Use "BastionXP CA" in articles, blog posts,
  talks, books, tutorials, comparisons and reviews, as long as it is clear
  you are not Ampas Labs.
- **Say what works with it.** For example "works with BastionXP CA" or
  "BastionXP CA integration for Ansible", as long as your own product has
  its own name.
- **Redistribute official releases.** Share or mirror unmodified releases
  and container images of BastionXP CA under its name.
- **Package it for an operating system.** Linux distributions, Homebrew
  and similar package managers may ship it as `bastionxp-ca`, including
  changes needed only to build, install or integrate it with the system
  (paths, service files, dependency versions, security fixes from upstream).
- **Describe a fork's origin.** Say your project is "based on BastionXP CA"
  or "a fork of BastionXP CA", as long as its own name is different.
- **Use the name in your own setup.** Name your internal servers,
  certificates and configuration whatever you like.

## You may not

- **Distribute a modified build under the Marks.** If you change the code
  beyond what packaging needs, give your version a different name that is
  not confusingly similar to BastionXP. A modified build must also change
  the default CA name (`ca.name`, "BastionXP"), so the certificates it
  issues are not named "BastionXP Root CA" and so on.
- **Use the Marks in your own names.** Do not use "BastionXP", or anything
  confusingly similar, in the name of a product, company, service, domain
  name or social media account.
- **Offer a hosted service under the Marks.** You may run BastionXP CA as
  a service for others, but do not call the service BastionXP or BastionXP
  CA, or suggest it is run or supported by Ampas Labs.
- **Suggest endorsement.** Do not use the Marks or logos in a way that
  suggests Ampas Labs sponsors, endorses or certifies you, your product or
  your service.
- **Change the logos.** If you use a logo where this policy allows it, use
  it as published, without changing its shape, colours or proportions.

## Anything else

If you are not sure whether your use is allowed, or want to do something
this policy does not allow, ask us at support@bastionxp.com.
